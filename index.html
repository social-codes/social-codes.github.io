Short gaming highlights from live streams

Social Codes publishes short, captioned highlight clips from live gaming streams to its own YouTube channel and Instagram account.

How it works

Our tool watches public live streams and notices moments where the audience reacts strongly. It prepares a short vertical clip with captions and credits the original streamer. A person on our team reviews every clip and decides whether to publish it.

Use of YouTube and Instagram

We use the YouTube API Services and the Instagram API only to upload videos to accounts that we own. We do not access, collect or store data from other YouTube or Instagram users. See our Privacy Policy and Terms of Service.

Creators

We clip creators who allow clipping, for example through official clipping programs. If you are a creator and want a clip removed, email us and we will take it down promptly.
