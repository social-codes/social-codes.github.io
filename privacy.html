Privacy Policy

Last updated: 18 November 2026

This policy explains how the Social Codes application ("we", "our") handles information. Social Codes is an internal tool that uploads short highlight videos to YouTube and Instagram accounts owned by Social Codes.

YouTube API Services

Social Codes uses YouTube API Services. By using our application you agree to the YouTube Terms of Service. Google's handling of data is described in the Google Privacy Policy.

Information we access

  • Our own YouTube channel: when the channel owner signs in, Google gives our application an authorization token for that channel. We use it only to upload videos and to read the channel's name to confirm the right channel is connected.
  • Our own Instagram account: an access token issued by Meta, used only to publish Reels to our account.
  • Public live-stream chat: our tool counts the number of public chat messages per few seconds to find highlight moments. Message counts are kept in memory only; we do not store chat messages, usernames or any other personal information.

We do not access, collect, store or process data about any other YouTube, Instagram or Twitch users.

How we use and store it

Authorization tokens are stored encrypted in Google Cloud Secret Manager and are used only by our upload service. Video clips are kept on our server temporarily and deleted after they are published or discarded, and in any case within 6 hours if not approved.

Sharing

We do not sell, rent or share any information with third parties. Videos are sent only to YouTube and Instagram for publishing.

Cookies

This website does not use cookies or tracking.

Revoking access

The account owner can revoke our application's access to a Google account at any time on the Google security settings page, and to an Instagram account in Instagram's settings under Apps and websites. When access is revoked or on request, we delete the stored tokens within 7 days.
